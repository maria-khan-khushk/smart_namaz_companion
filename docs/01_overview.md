# Awwab — Smart Namaz Companion
## Project Overview

---

### What Is Awwab?

**Awwab** (internally named `smart_namaz_companion`) is a cross-platform Islamic mobile application built with Flutter. It serves as an all-in-one digital companion for Muslims who want to maintain their daily prayer routine, track their consistency, learn Quranic supplications, and deepen their spiritual practice — all from a single, beautifully designed app.

The name *Awwab* (أَوَّاب) comes from Arabic, meaning "one who frequently returns to Allah" — a fitting name for an app designed to help Muslims stay connected to their five daily prayers.

---

### Mission Statement

> To make daily Islamic practice more accessible, consistent, and spiritually fulfilling through thoughtful technology.

---

### Target Audience

Awwab is designed for:

- Muslim individuals who want accurate, location-based prayer times on their phone
- Beginners learning how to perform Salah correctly
- Practicing Muslims who want to track their prayer streak and build discipline
- Anyone who wants a clean, distraction-free digital tasbih (prayer bead counter)
- Urdu-speaking users who prefer an app with full Urdu language support

---

### Core Value Proposition

Unlike generic prayer apps, Awwab is built around four pillars:

1. **Accuracy** — Prayer times are calculated in real time from the device's GPS location using the Aladhan API.
2. **Guidance** — Step-by-step instructions for each prayer (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) in both English and Urdu.
3. **Consistency** — A gamified streak tracker and daily prayer log encourage habit-building.
4. **Spirituality** — Built-in Tasbih counter, 99 Names of Allah, and daily Hadith keep the user spiritually engaged.
